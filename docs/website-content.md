Under this year’s theme, India Uncharted: A Nation of Multitudes, we hope to explore the diverse perspectives and opportunities shaping India’s future.

The Forum was born out of a shared desire among Indian international students at Harvard to remain meaningfully engaged with our nation amid profound change. As members of the Indian diaspora studying abroad, we see this gathering as an opportunity to contribute to India’s future through thoughtful conversations across disciplines, generations, and perspectives.

# Questions (of each Panel, more to come)

* Infra panel: As India grows and its economy changes, which infrastructure investments would do the most to expand access and support our population, and what will it take to deliver these investments?  
* Energy: What will it take to deliver clean power reliably and affordably for the India of tomorrow, and how should governments, companies, and consumers share the cost of getting there?  
* Women and Workforce: How can women shape the next chapter of India's economic growth, and what opportunities are emerging for greater participation, innovation, and leadership?  
* Literature: How does literature shape the stories a nation tells about itself, and how are Indian writers redefining those narratives today?  
* Law, Institutions and a Changing India: How can India's institutions balance continuity with change as the nation navigates new economic, technological, and social realities?
