# Harvard Undergraduate India Forum website

The public website for the Harvard Undergraduate India Forum: what the forum is, when and where it happens, who speaks, and how to take part.

## Language

**Harvard Undergraduate India Forum**:
The forum's full public name, used wherever the name appears in full.
_Avoid_: Harvard India Forum (the older wordmark), HUIF in public copy

**HUIF**:
Short internal name for the forum, used in code, file names and team conversation, not in visitor-facing headlines.

**Logo**:
The forum's own line-art mark: the torana drawing with the wordmark set between its pillars. It is the only identity mark the site uses.
_Avoid_: Harvard shield, Veritas, crest

**Gate**:
The real photographed east gateway of the Great Stupa at Sanchi that the visitor walks through on the site.
_Avoid_: Torana in visitor-facing copy, arch, door
